What we do

Our services

From routine servicing to complex engine work — handled by experienced mechanics using proper diagnostic equipment. All makes and models, petrol and diesel.

Full service & maintenance

Oil changes, filters, spark plugs, timing belts, and full inspection — keeping your vehicle in peak condition.

Engine diagnostics & repair

Diagnostic tools to identify faults fast. Cylinder head, gaskets, overhauls, and full engine repairs on all makes.

Brakes & suspension

Brake pads, discs, drums, callipers. Shock absorbers, tie rod ends, knuckles, and full suspension checks.

Electrical systems

Battery, alternators, starters, wiring faults, and all vehicle electrical diagnostics and repairs.

Cooling & radiator

Radiator service, thermostat, hoses, water pumps, coolant refill — preventing overheating before it starts.

Tyres & wheel alignment

Tyre fitting and balancing, puncture repairs, and wheel alignment for safe, even wear.

Gearbox & transmission

Manual and automatic gearbox servicing, clutch replacement, driveshaft, and differential repairs.

Air conditioning

Re-gas, leak detection, compressor repairs, and full AC system checks to keep your cabin cool.

Car towing

Stuck on the road? We offer vehicle towing to get you and your car safely to our workshop.
